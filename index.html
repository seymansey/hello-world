Polestar Choices

Polestar Choices, often known as Personal Contract Purchase, is a flexible form of loan where some of the cost is deferred until the end of the agreement to provide you with the benefit of lower monthly payments. The deferred amount is known as the Optional Final Payment. At the end of the agreement, you have three options: Renew, Retain, Return.
